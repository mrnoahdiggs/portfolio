Theatre

Immersive Class Design: The Classroom as a Stage

Drawing on my extensive background in programming multimedia theatre for young audiences — including those with diverse needs — I approach every lesson as a curated performance. I design classes integrating custom scripts, dynamic visual slides, and interactive activities that capture imaginations instantly and keep students highly engaged from the first bell to the last.

 Conducting

Bespoke Arrangements & Ensemble Leadership

My background in music composition allows me to directly serve the unique makeup of my ensembles. Whether leading a curricular class or an after-school CCA, I don't just buy sheet music; I arrange and adjust repertoire to match the exact instrumentation and skill levels of my students, ensuring every child feels successful and challenged.
