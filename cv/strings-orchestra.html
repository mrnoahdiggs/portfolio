Professional Summary

K–12 certified music educator and cellist with seven years of teaching experience across individual instruction, orchestral sectionals and school ensembles. I teach cello from beginner to advanced levels and violin, viola and double bass at beginner level, alongside experience in high-school strings co-direction and advanced chamber music. My approach uses individual listening during sectionals to guide coaching and repertoire adaptation, drawing on composition and arranging skills to respond to players’ technical readiness within ensemble work.
