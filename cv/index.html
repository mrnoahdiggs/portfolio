Professional Summary

K-12 certified music teacher with eight years teaching children aged 4 to 13, four of them in an IB PYP and US-standards international school. A cellist and composer by training, I came to Singapore in 2015 on a full scholarship to study Music Composition and have since built a practice that joins Orff, Kodály and Suzuki pedagogy with Universal Design for Learning and translanguaging, so that every child in a mixed-language classroom has a way in. Alongside classroom teaching I direct choirs and string ensembles, and I have written, directed and performed in multimedia arts programmes for two Esplanade festivals. Seeking an elementary or cross-divisional music role with a path towards leadership.

Teaching Philosophy

“The students looked very engaged and focused. Fun to watch!” — Elementary Deputy Principal, Stamford American International School

I create dynamic, highly engaging classrooms through multimodal instruction (visual, kinesthetic, auditory) that ensures every learning profile is supported. By establishing strong routines to reduce cognitive load, I empower students to focus entirely on mastering new concepts. My pedagogy is strictly aligned with developmental readiness, ranging from experiential, movement-based learning in early years to fast-released scaffolding and collaborative problem-solving in upper primary.
