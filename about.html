About Noah Diggs

“As an artist, educator, musician, and human, I strive to build beautiful worlds in order to teach us how to make our own a better place.”

As a mixed Filipino-American raised in the United States, I have always possessed a deep desire to broaden my perspective and learn from other cultures. From starting cello lessons at age 8 to beginning dance and movement classes at age 22, I am at my best when I am learning something new. This global mindset eventually led me across the planet to Singapore, where my focus shifted toward international pedagogy, music philanthropy, and arts production management.

Today, I strive to achieve a delicate balance in my classroom: building concrete instrumental skills, instilling an overall knowledge of musical concepts and history, and fostering the full enjoyment of music as an art form capable of inspiring awe and beauty.
